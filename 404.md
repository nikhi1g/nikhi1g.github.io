# 404 dot — life cycle plan

Planning doc for the status dot on [404.html](404.html). Nothing here is implemented
yet beyond what is listed under "Already shipped". Waiting on a reference image of the
stick figure before any of the sprout work starts.

## Already shipped

- Real physics: `pixelsPerMetre = 100`, `g = 981 px/s²`, restitution `0.58`, linear air
  drag, rolling resistance, sleep thresholds, fixed `1/240 s` timestep.
- The footer rule is a floor over its own x-span; past its end the floor is the card
  bottom, so the dot rolls off the edge on a proper arc.
- One random rightward shove chosen per load (`rollVector`), with drag scaled to it, is
  the only self-propelled motion. First `pointerdown` clears it forever, after which the
  dot goes only where it is thrown and can be rested on the rule.
- Draggable and throwable, with a clamped release velocity.
- **Eye**: sprouts only when the dot is fully asleep (`vx === 0 && vy === 0`), scale-in
  with overshoot, pupil tracks the cursor within a 1.5 px reach, and it blinks on a
  random 2.6–6.4 s cadence with a 25% chance of a double blink. Closes the moment the
  dot is in motion again.

## The idea

The ball is the creature's "curled up" state. When it comes to rest it sprouts into a
small stick figure. Disturb it — drag, throw, any motion — and it snaps back to a ball.
Left alone long enough, the figure gets bored and starts *chopping the page apart* with
a little axe, then puts it back together. It avoids the cursor the whole time.

## States

| state | trigger | look |
|---|---|---|
| `ball` | any motion (`vx !== 0 \|\| vy !== 0`) or being dragged | plain dot, no features |
| `resting` | physics asleep | dot + eye (shipped) |
| `sprouted` | asleep for ~1.2 s | stick figure, idle bob, eye tracking |
| `walking` | idle for ~6 s | figure walks along its current surface |
| `chopping` | reached a target element | axe swings, target degrades |
| `rebuilding` | page sufficiently broken, or ~20 s of chopping | reverses the damage |
| `fleeing` | cursor within ~90 px | drops the axe, runs/jumps away |

Any physics wake-up (drag, throw, or the surface disappearing under it) collapses
whatever is happening straight back to `ball`.

## Sprout animation

The pop is the piece to get right first, and the only thing to build in this pass.

- Limbs are four `<span>`s (two arms, two legs) plus the existing eye, all children of
  `.dot`, `pointer-events: none`.
- Each limb is a 1.5 px rounded bar drawn with `scaleY` from `0` at a `transform-origin`
  anchored on the ball's rim, so it grows *out of* the body rather than appearing.
- Order of the pop, total ~420 ms:
  1. body squashes `scaleX(1.18) scaleY(0.82)` for 90 ms — the wind-up
  2. legs shoot out on `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` with ~25 ms stagger
  3. arms follow 60 ms later, same curve
  4. body overshoots back through `scale(1.1)` and settles
- Reverse for the collapse, faster (~180 ms), limbs retracting before the body rounds.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: figure appears and disappears with opacity only, no bob, no
  walking, no chopping.

## Figure rig (from the reference drawing)

The ball is the **head**, and it is a cyclops — the existing single eye is the face, and
it stays. Everything is drawn in the dot's own 10 px coordinate space and scaled up as a
unit, so the eye and pupil scale with the head for free and the physics hitbox stays the
10 px ball sitting at the feet.

- **Head**: the dot itself, `--acc` blue, scaled ×2.2 → 22 px across when sprouted.
- **Eye**: large, roughly 60% of the head, pupil low and slightly right of centre.
- **Hair**: three bars off the crown, splayed about `-20° / 0° / +20°`, slightly curved,
  rounded caps.
- **Torso**: one straight vertical bar below the head, no joint.
- **Arms**: two bars from the upper torso, swept down and out ~35°, gently curved
  (noodle arms, no elbow), rounded caps.
- **Legs**: two straight bars splayed ~12° from the torso's base, each ending in a small
  angled foot.
- Limbs and hair are the light stroke colour (`--fg`), so they read against both themes.
- Standing offset: `transform-origin: 50% 100%` with
  `scale(2.2) translateY(-17.5px)`, which puts the feet exactly on the physics contact
  point and the head ~40 px above it.

## Walking

- Surfaces are the ones physics already knows: the footer rule's span and the card
  bottom. Walking is clamped to the current surface's x-range.
- Gait is a leg-swing animation driven off the same rAF loop as the physics, with
  `vx` set directly (~40 px/s) rather than through the force integrator.
- Reaching the end of the rule: it stops, peers over the edge, and either turns around
  or deliberately hops down to the floor below.
- Jump is a real physics impulse — set `vy` negative and hand control back to the
  integrator, so arcs match the rest of the simulation.

## Chopping

- Targets are real elements: the `h1`, the `.message` heading and paragraph, the `.hint`
  kbd, the footer rule, the footer text.
- A chop cycle is: walk to the target's x-position, ~3 axe swings, then apply one stage
  of damage. Damage stages per target (letters dropping off the heading, the rule
  splitting, the kbd border cracking) are CSS-driven and recorded in a log.
- Nothing is actually removed from the DOM — damage is class-based and fully reversible,
  so `rebuilding` just replays the log backwards. This also keeps the page readable and
  the return-home interactions intact at all times.
- Chopped letters that fall become physics bodies using the same integrator, landing on
  the rule or the floor.

## Cursor avoidance

- Cursor position is already tracked for the eye.
- Within ~90 px the figure enters `fleeing`: runs away along the surface, jumping the gap
  to the other surface if cornered.
- Between ~90 px and ~180 px it keeps working but watches the cursor — eye tracking plus
  a body lean away from it.
- Fleeing never overrides the click-anywhere-to-go-home behaviour; the figure is
  `pointer-events: none` except for the ball's own drag handle.

## Architecture

`404.html` is a thin shell — markup plus three stylesheet links and one module script.
GitHub Pages only serves `/404.html`, so the shell has to stay at the repo root; every
component lives in `404/` and is copied into `_site` by
[.github/scripts/build-site.mjs](.github/scripts/build-site.mjs).

| file | owns |
|---|---|
| [404/css/base.css](404/css/base.css) | theme tokens, body, card, header, type, links, footer |
| [404/css/interaction.css](404/css/interaction.css) | copy toast, commit span, hint line, leave animation |
| [404/css/figure.css](404/css/figure.css) | dot, SVG rig, joints, eye, blink, sprout |
| [404/js/theme.js](404/js/theme.js) | `initTheme()` — dark/light toggle and persistence |
| [404/js/clipboard.js](404/js/clipboard.js) | `initClipboard()` — copy toast, email/commit/version copy |
| [404/js/return-home.js](404/js/return-home.js) | `initReturnHome()` — click/key to leave |
| [404/js/version.js](404/js/version.js) | `initVersionWatcher()` — `commit.json` polling |
| [404/js/physics.js](404/js/physics.js) | `createDot()` — the whole simulation |
| [404/js/figure.js](404/js/figure.js) | `initFigure(dot)` — eye, blinking, sprout/collapse |
| [404/js/main.js](404/js/main.js) | wiring, nothing else |

Modules share no globals. The only cross-module contract is the object `createDot()`
returns: `{ el, start(), onSleepChange(fn), isAsleep(), isDragging() }`. Physics owns
motion and the `asleep` class; it knows nothing about eyes, limbs, or blinking. The
creature subscribes to `onSleepChange` and owns everything expressive. Any new behaviour
(walking, axe, damage log, rebuild) should be its own module behind that same contract,
so the phases below can be built in parallel without touching each other's files.

## Constraints

- No dependencies, no bundler, no build step beyond the existing copy script. Plain CSS
  and ES modules.
- All of it is CSS/SVG transforms plus the existing rAF loop — no canvas, no WebGL.
- The rig is stroke-based SVG so it stays crisp at any size; never scale a small raster
  or a sub-pixel CSS box to fake size.
- The page stays a working 404 first: click anywhere / press any key to return home,
  theme toggle, commit copy, and email copy all keep working untouched.

## Build order

1. **Sprout/collapse animation only** — limbs, pop, reverse, reduced-motion path. Stop
   here for review.
2. Idle life: bob, weight shift, occasional look-around.
3. Walking along both surfaces, plus the hop between them.
4. Cursor avoidance.
5. Axe and the reversible damage log.
6. Rebuild pass.
